Changes: scan every published row; select exact supplied person names/QIDs; map provider occupation labels to dictionary topics; add a historical topic for supplied birth years before 1900. No HPI/rank/count cutoff, synthesized names or name splitting. Broad occupations do not establish a narrower sport or Olympic participation. Upstream classification is not an independent career audit.
Raw dates, locations, coordinates, scores and social accounts remain outside the dictionary. Adult-industry records remain in curation and are excluded from distribution by the pinned policy.
